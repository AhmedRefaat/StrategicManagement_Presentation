# Presentation Content and Speaker Plan

The horizontal route is designed for a maximum 15-minute presentation. Vertical slides contain detailed evidence for questions.

## Main sequence

1. Company foundation
2. External environment
3. Internal environment
4. Strategy formulation and choice
5. Business-level strategy
6. Functional strategy and implementation
7. Advisor relevance
8. Critical assessment and limitations
9. Conclusion and Q&A

## Presenter split

- Ahmed: opening through strategy formulation and selection, approximately 7 minutes.
- Fatma: business-level strategy through implementation, risks, and closing, approximately 7 minutes.
- Use one handover after the strategic choice.

## Handover

“The market offers real growth, but the decisive question is whether Al Rawae is internally ready to capture it. Fatma will now show how the chosen strategy converts the diagnosis into a controlled implementation plan.”

## Presentation principle

Use the right arrow for the executive story and the down arrow only when detailed evidence is requested.
